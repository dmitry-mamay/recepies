ingredients:
  - title: For the meatballs
    items:
      - item: ground meat
        amount: 500 g
        note: pork and beef, 1:1
      - item: medium onion
        amount: "1"
      - item: garlic
        amount: 2 cloves
      - item: finely chopped parsley
        amount: 4 tbsp
      - item: breadcrumbs
        amount: 50 g
      - item: egg
        amount: "1"
      - item: nutmeg
        amount: ½ tsp
      - item: olive oil
        note: for frying
  - title: For the tomato sauce
    items:
      - item: passata
        amount: 300 g
        note: or 400 g tomato juice
      - item: garlic
        amount: 2 cloves
      - item: cayenne pepper
        amount: 1 tsp
      - item: white wine vinegar
        amount: 1 tbsp
        note: about 6% acidity
      - item: olive oil
        amount: 1 tbsp
steps:
  - title: Make the tomato sauce
    text: Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a small saucepan over high heat. Add the finely chopped garlic for the sauce and cayenne pepper; cook for 1–2 minutes, until strongly fragrant. Pour in the passata and add the vinegar. Simmer, stirring occasionally, until it reaches the desired thickness.
    image: ./images/step-1.jpg
    imageAlt: Tomato sauce simmering in a saucepan with a wooden spoon
  - title: Fry the onion and garlic
    text: Finely chop the onion and mince the garlic for the meatballs. Fry them over high heat until the onion is translucent. For a deeper flavor, cook until lightly caramelized.
    image: ./images/step-2.jpg
    imageAlt: Chopped onion and garlic frying in a skillet
  - title: Mix the meat mixture
    text: In a large bowl, combine the ground meat, parsley, nutmeg, breadcrumbs, egg, and fried onion and garlic. Mix well until evenly combined.
    image: ./images/step-3.jpg
    imageAlt: Ground meat, egg, breadcrumbs, herbs, and spices in a mixing bowl
  - title: Shape the meatballs
    text: Shape the mixture into small balls about the size of walnuts. You should get about 15 meatballs. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
    image: ./images/step-4.jpg
    imageAlt: Raw round meatballs arranged on a cutting board
  - title: Brown the meatballs
    text: Heat olive oil in a skillet over high heat. Brown the meatballs in batches, leaving space between them, for 1–2 minutes on each side. Do not overcrowd the pan or turn them constantly. Browning should take about 10 minutes in total.
    image: ./images/step-5.jpg
    imageAlt: Browned meatballs frying in a skillet
  - title: Cook through
    text: After browning, cook the meatballs for another 6–8 minutes. Cooking time depends on their size; check the center for doneness. The safe internal temperature for ground meat is 71 °C.
    image: ./images/step-6.jpg
    imageAlt: Browned meatballs simmering in tomato sauce in a skillet
  - title: Finish with the sauce and serve
    text: Once all the meatballs are cooked, return them to the skillet, pour over the tomato sauce, and heat together for 2 minutes. Serve hot, ideally with thick spaghetti. Garnish with fresh parsley and freshly ground black pepper, if desired.
    image: ./images/step-7.jpg
    imageAlt: Meatballs in tomato sauce served with spaghetti and parsley
tips:
  - Do not skip chilling the meat mixture for 30 minutes; it helps the meatballs brown well and stay juicy.
  - Do not put all the meatballs in the skillet at once, or they will steam instead of brown.
  - Do not keep stirring the meatballs; let each side brown.
  - Cooking time depends on the size of the meatballs. Check the center; ground meat is safe at 71 °C.
