subtitle: Delicate mascarpone dessert
description: Tiramisu is a classic Italian dessert with airy mascarpone, egg, and sugar cream, coffee-soaked savoiardi, and a light dusting of cocoa. Easy to prepare, but it needs time to chill.
image: ./images/hero.jpg
imageAlt: A slice of tiramisu with cream, coffee-soaked savoiardi, and cocoa on a ceramic plate
servings:
  min: 6
  max: 8
  note: 20×20 cm dish
prepMinutes: 30
cookMinutes: 0
restMinutes: 360
restNote: at least 6 hours, preferably overnight
tags:
  - десерт
  - итальянская-кухня
ingredientsImage: ./images/ingredients.jpg
ingredientsImageAlt: Mascarpone, eggs, sugar, coffee, savoiardi, cocoa, and rum on a wooden table
ingredients:
  - title: For the cream
    items:
      - item: mascarpone
        amount: 250 g
      - item: eggs
        amount: "3"
        note: separate the yolks and whites
      - item: sugar
        amount: 70 g
  - title: For soaking
    items:
      - item: strong coffee
        amount: 200 ml
        note: espresso or very strong coffee
      - item: sugar
        amount: 2 tbsp
        note: optional; can be replaced with rum or cognac
  - title: Also needed
    items:
      - item: savoiardi
        amount: 24–28
        note: or ladyfingers for tiramisu
      - item: cocoa powder
        note: for dusting
steps:
  - title: Whisk the yolks and sugar
    text: Whisk the egg yolks and sugar in a bowl until pale and fluffy, for 3–5 minutes. The mixture should increase in volume and lighten in color.
    image: ./images/step-1.jpg
    imageAlt: Egg yolks and sugar in a glass bowl with a whisk
  - title: Add the mascarpone
    text: Gently fold the mascarpone into the yolk mixture until smooth. Do not whisk for too long, or the cream may become runny.
    image: ./images/step-2.jpg
    imageAlt: Mascarpone and egg yolk cream in a glass bowl with a spatula
  - title: Whip the egg whites
    text: In a separate clean bowl, whip the egg whites until stiff peaks form. They should be firm, glossy, and stay in place when the bowl is turned over.
    image: ./images/step-3.jpg
    imageAlt: Egg whites whipped to stiff peaks with mixer beaters
  - title: Fold the cream together
    text: Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the cream in 2–3 batches using a spatula, moving from the bottom upward. Take care not to deflate the mixture.
    image: ./images/step-4.jpg
    imageAlt: Whipped egg whites being folded into mascarpone cream with a spatula
  - title: Prepare the base
    text: Quickly dip both sides of each savoiardo in the coffee. Do not leave them in too long, or they will become soggy. Add sugar, rum, or cognac to the coffee if desired.
    image: ./images/step-5.jpg
    imageAlt: A savoiardo biscuit dipped in a bowl of strong coffee
  - title: Assemble the tiramisu
    text: Arrange a layer of biscuits in the dish, followed by a layer of cream. Repeat the layers, usually 2–3 layers, finishing with cream. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight.
    image: ./images/step-6.jpg
    imageAlt: Layered tiramisu assembled in a square glass dish
  - title: Dust with cocoa and serve
    text: Just before serving, sift cocoa powder over the tiramisu. Cut into portions and enjoy.
    image: ./images/step-7.jpg
    imageAlt: A slice of finished tiramisu dusted with cocoa, next to a sieve
tips:
  - Use fresh pasteurized eggs — the classic recipe contains raw eggs.
  - Do not soak the savoiardi too much; they should stay slightly firm.
  - For a richer flavor, add a little rum or cognac to the coffee.
  - Tiramisu is best enjoyed the next day, when it becomes softer and more aromatic.
