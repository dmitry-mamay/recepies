steps:
  - title: Marinate the chicken
    text: Mix the yogurt, lemon juice, ginger, garlic, salt, and all the spices for the chicken. Add the chicken pieces and mix well. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, preferably 4–12 hours.
    image: ./images/step-1.jpg
    imageAlt: Pieces of chicken in a spiced yogurt marinade
  - title: Brown the chicken
    text: Heat a skillet with a small amount of oil. Brown the chicken in batches over high or medium-high heat. It does not need to be fully cooked; it will finish cooking in the sauce. Transfer to a plate.
    image: ./images/step-2.jpg
    imageAlt: Pieces of marinated chicken browned in a skillet
  - title: Make the tomato base
    text: Melt the butter with the vegetable oil in the same skillet. Cook the finely chopped onion for 5–7 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for another minute. Add the garam masala, cumin, coriander, and paprika; stir and heat for 30 seconds. Add the tomatoes, a little salt, and sugar to taste. Simmer over low heat for 15–20 minutes.
    image: ./images/step-3.jpg
    imageAlt: Tomato base with cooked onion and spices in a skillet
  - title: Blend the sauce
    text: Blend the sauce with an immersion blender until smooth. If it is too thick, add a little water. For an especially smooth texture, pass it through a sieve.
    image: ./images/step-4.jpg
    imageAlt: Tomato sauce being blended smooth with an immersion blender
  - title: Simmer the chicken in the sauce
    text: Return the chicken to the skillet and simmer over low heat for 8–12 minutes until fully cooked. Pour in the cream and warm for another 2–3 minutes without bringing it to a vigorous boil. Rub the dried fenugreek leaves between your palms and add them to the sauce. Taste and adjust the salt, sugar, and spices.
    image: ./images/step-5.jpg
    imageAlt: Pieces of chicken in a smooth creamy tomato sauce with herbs
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve Butter Chicken hot with basmati rice or garlic naan. Add a little fresh cilantro and a pat of butter if desired.
    image: ./images/step-6.jpg
    imageAlt: Butter Chicken served with basmati rice, naan, and fresh cilantro
tips:
  - Butter and cream give the dish its characteristic rich flavor.
  - Garam masala is the key spice blend. Do not replace it with curry powder if you want the distinctive Indian flavor.
  - Kasuri methi, or dried fenugreek leaves, provides one of the signature aromas of restaurant-style Butter Chicken.
  - Cook down the tomatoes well before adding the cream so the sauce is not watery or overly acidic.
  - Chicken thighs usually stay juicier than chicken breast.
