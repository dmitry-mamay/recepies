subtitle: White bean salad with onion, tomatoes, and egg
description: Piyaz is a traditional Turkish salad, especially popular in the Aegean region. It combines simple ingredients, bright flavor, and freshness. Tender white beans, red onion, tomatoes, boiled eggs, and a fragrant olive oil and lemon dressing create a balanced taste.

ingredients:
  - title: For the salad
    items:
      - item: white beans
        amount: 400 g
        note: canned or cooked
      - item: medium tomatoes
        amount: "2"
      - item: red onion
        amount: "1"
      - item: boiled eggs
        amount: "4"
      - item: parsley
        amount: 1 small bunch
        note: or a mix of parsley and dill
  - title: For the dressing
    items:
      - item: olive oil
        amount: 4 tbsp
        note: Extra Virgin
      - item: lemon juice
        amount: 2 tbsp
        note: freshly squeezed
      - item: garlic clove
        amount: "1"
        note: optional
      - item: salt
        amount: ½ tsp
        note: or to taste
      - item: black pepper
        amount: ¼ tsp
        note: or to taste
      - item: dried oregano
        amount: ½ tsp
        note: optional
  - title: Also needed
    items:
      - item: red pepper flakes or paprika
        amount: a pinch
        note: optional
      - item: lemon wedges and a little olive oil
        note: for serving; drizzle the oil over the top
steps:
  - title: Prepare the beans
    text: If using dried beans, soak them overnight and cook until tender. If using canned beans, rinse under cold water and drain. Cooking beans that are not already prepared takes 10–15 minutes.
    image: ./images/step-1.jpg
    imageAlt: White beans in a clear glass bowl
  - title: Chop the ingredients
    text: Cut the tomatoes into small cubes, thinly slice the onion into half-rings, and finely chop the parsley. Boil the eggs for 8–10 minutes, cool, peel, and cut into quarters or halves.
    image: ./images/step-2.jpg
    imageAlt: Thinly sliced red onion in a glass bowl
  - title: Make the dressing
    text: In a small bowl, mix the olive oil, freshly squeezed lemon juice, minced garlic, salt, pepper, and oregano. For a little heat, add a pinch of red pepper flakes or paprika.
    image: ./images/step-3.jpg
    imageAlt: Olive oil with garlic and herbs in a glass bowl with a whisk
  - title: Mix the salad
    text: Gently combine the beans, tomatoes, onion, and herbs in a large bowl. Pour over the dressing and mix well.
    image: ./images/step-4.jpg
    imageAlt: White beans, chopped tomatoes, red onion, and herbs in a glass bowl
  - title: Add the eggs
    text: Arrange the boiled egg wedges on top. If desired, add a little olive oil and a little more oregano or paprika.
    image: ./images/step-5.jpg
    imageAlt: Boiled egg wedges on top of a white bean, tomato, and herb salad
  - title: Serve
    text: Let the salad stand for 5–10 minutes and serve chilled. It goes well with crusty bread or pita; add lemon wedges and a little olive oil when serving.
    image: ./images/step-6.jpg
    imageAlt: Finished bean salad with eggs, herbs, and a lemon wedge in a ceramic bowl
tips:
  - For a fuller flavor, use genuine Extra Virgin olive oil and freshly squeezed lemon juice.
  - Red onion is a traditional ingredient in Piyaz, giving the salad bright flavor and color.
  - You can also use cannellini beans, lentils, or chickpeas.
  - For a bolder flavor, add a little Turkish hot pepper (aci biber) or paprika.
